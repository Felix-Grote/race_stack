F2 follow-up: bounded evaluation arithmetic and fixed Gauss32 constants

The previous128*eps*coefficient_scale evaluation allowance was deliberately broad,
but it exceeded the entire per-segment policy budget on two pre-existing A03
negative-candidate constructions, so those tests could not reach their intended
chart checks. No tolerance or resource limit is changed. The separate128eps
Bezier-box and positive derivative-bound arithmetic inflation remains unchanged.

The Gauss32 roots/weights are now correctly rounded fixed binary64 constants.
The retained generator evaluates Legendre recurrence/Newton roots and weights at
100 Decimal digits; its full decimal records and degree0..63 moment checks are
retained. This removes numerical uncertainty from a runtime numpy eigensolve.
Positive roots/weights are mirrored to[0,1]; power-of-two weight scaling is exact.

Let S=|c1|+2|c2|+3|c3|, where |.| denotes Euclidean vector norm. Under the declared
usual binary64 model with correctly rounded basic arithmetic and <=2eps hypot:

* Horner derivative evaluation uses at most6eps*S absolute error to first order
  (the multiplication by2 is exact; multiplication/addition chain has at most5
  other rounded operations;6 provides headroom).
* Correctly rounded roots and the[0,1] map, then lo+(hi-lo)*node, give at most
  3eps absolute parameter error. Since |d_t'|<=2*S, its contribution is<=6eps*S.
* Hypot contributes<=2eps*S.
* Correctly rounded weights, weight*speed, positive math.fsum and final h product
  contribute<=4eps*S after multiplication byh.

Their first-order total<=18eps*S*h is covered by32eps*S*h, leaving14eps of
headroom for higher-order terms and scale evaluation. The final sum of panel
values still has its separate4eps*abs(value) allowance. This is an explicit
software arithmetic model, not a machine-checked IEEE or physical certificate.
The analytic Gauss remainder, nonzero-derivative boxes and feature refinement
remain separate from this numerical evaluation allowance.

Regression-first evidence: two tight-budget coefficients copied from unchanged
A03 tests fail before the follow-up (f2-followup-red.xml/log). New reference tests
require their construction and independent512/1024-point Gaussian agreement;
existing A03 tests stay unchanged. A fixed Gauss degree0..63 moment regression
checks the production constants. Full reference+two original A03 candidate
checks and independent adversarial replay provide the final verification.
