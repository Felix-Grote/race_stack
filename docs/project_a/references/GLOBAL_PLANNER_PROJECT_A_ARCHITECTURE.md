## 1. Scope, identity and evidence

Deliver one manually selected, approved complete route → one ordered whole-lap minimum-curvature trajectory → cyclic speed profile → independently validated immutable reference and legacy waypoint view. Support legal borders without walls, selected split/remerge alternatives, explicit ramps and disconnected surfaces coincident in XY. Alternatives are separate manifests, not an online graph or route search.

Project A owns offline geometry, numerical assumptions, static initialization/completion metadata and stateless progress queries. **Excluded:** live pose/occurrence tracking, RouteState/freshness, controllers, command authorization, runtime behavior/recovery, perception, driver changes, deployment and physical validation. A surface label is supplied physical identity, not an inferred altitude. Initial ramp support is planar geometry plus explicitly supplied conservative limits; no grade/contact/tire guarantee is inferred.

### Exact adapter boundary (**D**)

`iqp_route_adapter.optimize(prepared, original_route, solver_policy) -> CandidateCurve + correspondence + iteration_report`:

1. Validate original directed occurrence/portal topology. Fit a periodic ordered seed curve; reject degenerate/unsmooth/legal-incompatible preparation rather than silently increasing widths. Carry an unwrapped ordinal seed coordinate q (piecewise seed arclength), never recovered by whole-route XY projection.
2. Integrate that curve and resample for near-equal solver spacing; carry `(old_segment,t,q)` monotonically. Build TUM matrices/normals. For every new reference station, intersect its normal with **only its occurrence or explicit adjacent portal support**, selecting the connected legal interval containing the reference. Convert left-positive offset a to TUM right-positive alpha=−a. Require finite signed widths and local chart `1−kappa*a >= epsilon_chart` throughout the usable interval.
3. Call unchanged `opt_min_curv`. Retain early damping and linearization-error criterion from stock IQP; bound QP count, elapsed time, point count and finite/conditioning checks. A timeout/infeasibility is failure, not permission to relax legality.
4. Form next periodic cubic through shifted ordered knots (`calc_splines`/`create_raceline` coefficients). Carry q by segment/t inheritance, then accurately resample and **recompute** normal intervals against original legal geometry/new normals. Never accumulate `width ± alpha`, globally nearest-match a border, or allow smoothing to reconnect coincident surfaces.
5. Final geometry is the actual shifted cubic, not a fresh fit to its exported samples. Resolve each gate by a unique directed intersection within its inherited q bracket; require ordered gate crossings. Ambiguous/missing portal intersection or ownership reversal rejects the candidate. Inherited labels alone are not proof of passage.

Earliest decisive implementation experiment: asymmetric, independently fixed S3 occurrence corridors with a narrow local border/notch and explicit portals; instrument every resample's q and normal owner, compare recomputed widths to stock transported widths, and independently check the final swept footprint. Pair with S0 equation/regression equivalence on identical prepared input. If failure is reference/chart quality, try a better seed or narrower valid chart first. Escalate to C only for a **demonstrated** legal feasible path not representable by the validated local chart, or measured unmanageable dense-matrix scaling—not merely a crossing or unavailable physical evidence.

Each occurrence's legal ribbon is a locally simple polygon formed by its two directed boundary chains and end caps. Split/remerge alternatives specify their selected ribbon separately. End caps delimit ownership, not physical walls: explicit portal support permits the finite footprint to span only declared adjacent regions. Global projected ribbons may overlap across distinct occurrences/surfaces; only occurrence-owned polygons/obstacles are consulted. Reject inconsistent side orientation, invalid simple polygons, disconnected normal intervals and unsupported local chart folding. More complex legal holes must be explicitly represented or rejected; do not fill them silently.

**Canonical representation decision:** persist the **final cubic coefficients**, not just waypoints. This reuses the solver's actual curve and avoids re-fitting/version-sensitive interpolation changing the validated path. Persisting a large numerical arc-inversion table is unnecessary. Integrate `|r_i'(t)|` with a specified error-budgeted kernel; derive segment cumulative lengths once in the loader; invert monotonically using bracketed root finding plus bounded integration. An in-memory acceleration table may be built, but is not a second curve/authority. Numerical policy and kernel identity are hashed. Require query error and loaded total length agreement within recorded bounds; do not promise bit-identical metric coordinates across arbitrary numerical platforms. Artifact digest covers exact coefficients and policies, not approximate solver reproducibility.

Speed/gate anchors use segment/t to avoid storing both metric s and another segment coordinate as authorities. Loader derives their s on the canonical curve; knots must be unique/increasing, first at seam, u finite/nonnegative. Squared speed is linear in **actual arc length** between consecutive knots including closing edge. Acceleration is `(u_j-u_i)/(2 delta_s_i)`, piecewise with an explicit right-hand-at-knot convention; heading/curvature derive from the same coefficients. No index×0.1, chord length substituted for arc length, or second spline fitted by consumers. Validation summary L/time may be recorded, but must agree with recomputation, not override it.

JSON: reject duplicate keys, nonfinite numbers, unsupported versions and invalid types/units; normalize IDs/numbers and negative zero. SHA-256 canonical UTF-8 `json.dumps(payload, sort_keys=True, separators=(',', ':'), ensure_ascii=False, allow_nan=False)` under a documented serializer contract. Digest excludes its own field and nonbehavioral wall-clock metadata; timings/iteration traces belong in a separate run report, not the identity payload. Include original geometry/model or content-addressed, verified attachments sufficient for independent revalidation; no unresolved filename-only inputs. Write only accepted artifacts by atomic rename into new digest directories; refuse overwrite. Loader verifies H/policy and returns immutable records. Changing coefficients/caps/model/margins/topology/kernel policy changes H.

## 5. Finalization and independent validator contract (**D**)

### Dynamics: smallest defensible first model

Use a conservative **planar** squared-speed LP, not a new tire OCP or minimum-time solver. For edge i→j, actual `delta_s>0`, `a=(u_j-u_i)/(2 delta_s)`, including last→first. Apply per-occurrence/metric static caps **before** retiming; split edges at cap, model, ownership and steering-bound changes. Include the applicable limits of both adjacent supports at portals. Optimize distance-weighted u; this is **not minimum lap time**.

First supported envelope: explicit speed range and conservative constant positive/negative longitudinal, lateral and motor limits per region. Tabulated GGV/motor inputs can supply minima over the entire allowed speed range with a recorded interpolation rule—no unverified tangent-majorant or extrapolation needed. Let c=drag/mass, K_i bound |curvature| throughout the edge. At both endpoints enforce speed caps, motor `a+c*u <= M`, and the sufficient diamond inequalities
`+(a+c*u)/Ax_pos + K_i*u/Ay <= 1` and `-(a+c*u)/Ax_neg + K_i*u/Ay <= 1`.
These imply the declared combined budget at edge interiors because a is constant, u affine, and K_i is a whole-edge bound. Integration/rounding uncertainty is included: enforce the sufficient inequalities at both bounded delta_s extremes as well as both u endpoints, with outward error allowances; the validator recomputes those bounds. Conservative != tire truth. A less conservative speed-dependent envelope is added only if this model rejects useful cases and its sufficient bound is independently demonstrated.

Optional supplied wheelbase/steering limits: check `delta=atan(wheelbase*kappa)` and bound `|d delta/ds|*v` over each edge; impose derived speed caps before retiming. Explicit speed-reference slew limits may further restrict a; they are not grip measurements. Missing required model/calibration means UNSUPPORTED, not fabricated defaults. A finite moving-lap policy rejects zero-speed intervals/nontraversable profiles; startup, dwell, stopping and hill-hold are external.

### Validator inputs and results

Inputs: **actual final coefficients/profile**, independently supplied approved legal/physical geometry, footprint/model/margins, policy/tolerances. Output: structured PASS / REJECT / INCONCLUSIVE / UNSUPPORTED with worst residuals, offending parameter intervals, assumptions and coverage. Only PASS for all required policy checks permits artifact publication. A synthetic planar PASS explicitly excludes missing contact/3D/physical coverage. Validator recomputes values; it never trusts QP convergence, stored headings, width arrays, speed metadata or legacy checker warnings.

Minimum checks:

- Finite coefficients; endpoint position/unit tangent/signed curvature continuity at **every join and seam**; bounded nonzero derivative norm; metric L and positive arc intervals; valid q/occurrence order; unique directed gate crossings and correct final→first transition. Coincident nonadjacent XY is legal, not connectivity.
- Signed containment against original occurrence-owned legal polygons, footprint length/width/overhangs and separately specified margins; explicit portal support, physical obstacles only on applicable surfaces. A rectangle needs edge/interior/hole checks, not just four inside corners.
- **Between-sample guarantee under a stated numerical model:** split at coefficient/profile/ownership/portal boundaries. For an interval of length h around its midpoint, reference-aligned footprint displacement is bounded by `b <= (1+R*K)*h/2 + epsilon_numeric` (R maximum footprint radius, K certified interval curvature bound). Accept the whole interval only if midpoint footprint's clearance to the legal/physical complement exceeds b plus required margins; otherwise subdivide. Use cubic/Bézier derivative bounds with outward-rounded arithmetic/error allowances to bound regularity/K, and robust polygon predicates with explicit error allowances. No bound or exhausted subdivision budget => INCONCLUSIVE/reject. Midpoint dense sampling alone cannot grant PASS. External pose/yaw tracking errors are only covered if a supplied margin/model actually bounds them.
- Independently recompute whole-edge speed/combined acceleration/machine/optional steering constraints using canonical arc and curvature bounds; closing edge mandatory. Reconstruct metadata/finite reference time. Injected narrow border excursions and interior curvature peaks must reject even when endpoints pass.
- Digest, units/+X CCW sign, model/geometry provenance and derived exports agree; post-validation geometry/profile mutation prohibited. Use a separate high-accuracy integration/differentiation oracle in tests to avoid two callers sharing the same metric-kernel bug.

**Legacy checker repair:** `OPT/helper_funcs_glob/src/check_traj.py:57–61` takes `[0]` from local `interp_track`, which returns an Nx4 ndarray. This reduces each boundary to one four-value row, not a full XY curve. Remove truncation, enforce Nx2 boundary shapes in a regression and retain warning-only behavior for baseline characterization. Even repaired, its unsigned corner-to-boundary-sample distances (1 m sampling), own reconstructed borders, no occurrence ownership, global maxima of acceleration tables and warning-only output cannot certify signed/swept legal containment or continuous dynamics. New validator does not call it.

## 6. Static downstream contract and compatibility (**D**)

`sample(s)` accepts finite metric progress, wraps periodically and returns H, wrapped s, curve segment/t, occurrence/surface or explicit portal support, XY, unit tangent/left normal, +X CCW heading, signed curvature, v and edge acceleration. `locate(s)` and `window` use the same arc kernel; seam sample is defined once, no implicit endpoint clipping. At ownership/profile boundaries use a documented half-open/right-hand convention and expose adjacent support where needed. No XY→occurrence selection, mutable closest index, history, lap counting or validity. State-estimation/controller teams own every live association/preview/authorization decision. They receive H, frame/units, curve/anchors, gates/init metadata, model/coverage limitations and exact query tolerances.

Legacy Wpnt export derives all fields from final canonical queries; `d_m=0`; `d_left/right` are occurrence-conditioned geometric distances, not full-footprint clearance certificates. Emit unique samples plus checked terminal duplicate at s=L, converting heading once (TPH north-zero only at its boundary). Compute `est_lap_time` from the main final speed profile. Reuse nine-group JSON writer in a **new output directory**; empty optional centerline/SP groups explicitly mean absent, never label the main route as a shortest-path solution. Existing republisher can read that view; test deserialization without starting nodes. Existing archived maps/JSONs remain untouched, simple-track extraction remains legacy. Exact numerical equality to old unsafe widths/speed is not required; compare equations on equal preparation and final feasibility under equal assumptions.

## 7. Few remaining gates and adversarial conclusion

1. **Blocks trusted A2 IQP results, not A1 schema work:** demonstrate inherited correspondence + recomputed original-corridor widths + independent swept validity on asymmetric S3 and portal fixtures. Direct stock IQP success does not settle it.
2. **Blocks continuous validation release:** implement/test conservative numerical regularity/clearance/curvature bounds and fail-closed subdivision. Polygon topology/contact coverage cannot be guessed from occupancy gaps.
3. **Blocks final speed guarantees:** select explicit supported planar vehicle budgets, then verify cyclic LP and steering model on final metric geometry. Real tire/grade/contact calibration limits physical claims, not synthetic implementation.
4. **Blocks competition-scale claim:** TPH builds/inverts dense 4N×4N matrices. At N=1200 (roughly 240 m/.2 m), one such float64 matrix alone is ~184 MB; several matrices and cubic-time linear algebra are used. No S6 timing/memory experiment here. Try a validated coarser solver mesh with fine export first; require measured scaling before sparse-algebra changes or C.
5. **Blocks authentic repaired-legacy full pipeline comparison:** scalar-objective helper defect, checker shape defect, TkAgg/stack_master coupling and asset metadata (`initial_pose`, `map_editor_mode`) need isolated, explicitly labeled comparator treatment. None requires runtime subsystem repairs or prevents a new pure planner CLI.

Adversarial checks: the selected list is simpler than a graph; coefficients avoid a second reconstructed curve, while inversion tables/derived arrays are omitted. No new shared runtime framework. No operation infers a connection from XY. Ordered resampling is necessary but not sufficient: smoothing, changing normals and cubic overshoot are checked against original geometry. Whole-edge bounds—not dense samples—control PASS. Speed/caps/metadata refer to the final serialized cubic. Raw helper shape failure belongs to preprocessing, not the QP; stock IQP does not perform global nearest lookup. Unknown physical clearance and unmeasured dynamics remain declared limits. Project A assigns no runtime tasks to itself. The first functionality PR can deliver useful route validation independently of all unresolved IQP/vehicle integration questions.
